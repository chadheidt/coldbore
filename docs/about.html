Loadscope facts

Loadscope is reloading software for Mac and Windows that scores the load-development tests you already shot and names your best load.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

What it is

Loadscope is a desktop program for Mac and Windows that scores the load-development tests you have already shot and names your best load. It reads your chronograph file and your target data, scores every powder charge, seating depth or tuner setting you tested, and shows the winner and why it won.

What it reads

  • Chronographs: Garmin Xero, LabRadar, MagnetoSpeed and Athlon Rangecraft files.
  • Target apps: BallisticX, OnTarget, ShotMarker and Silver Mountain exports. You can also measure your groups inside Loadscope from a target photo.

What it scores

Velocity flat-spot, velocity SD, mean radius and vertical SD, combined into one composite score. If your target data has no mean radius, group size stands in for it. Loadscope also predicts your DOPE from 100 to 1000 yards off your measured velocity and prints a DOPE Card and a one-page Recipe Card.

What it does not do

  • It does not predict pressure.
  • It does not contain published load data and it never tells you what charge to load. It ranks the loads you tested. Always work up from your manual.

Price and terms

  • US$79, one time (US$59 through December 31, 2026). Free updates. No subscription. Installs on up to two of your computers, works offline and needs no account.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee. Refund policy.
  • Loadscope Ballistics for iPhone and iPad is free to download; its unlock is included when you own the desktop program, or US$9.99 on its own.

Platforms

Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows. Minimum system versions are shown in the download window on the homepage. The program is in English.

Who makes it

Loadscope LLC, an Oregon company. Out of beta since June 29, 2026. Questions: support@loadscope.app. Every release is listed in the release notes.
