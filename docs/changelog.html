LOADSCOPE™

Release notes

Every Loadscope release since launch, newest first. Updates are free for every owner, and the app tells you when one is ready.

Version 1.17.7

  • Loadscope now runs on a newer version of Python, the language it's built in. Nothing changes in how you use it.

Version 1.17.6

  • New Ask a question button: type a question in your own words and get the answer, right in the app.
  • The Recipe Card has an optional Case prep box for your brass numbers.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.17.5

  • Range & DOPE now remembers the temperature and altitude you set, and your printed DOPE card uses them too.
  • Starting a different kind of test now asks one clear question, and brings your bullet along to the new load.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.17.4

  • Adding your chronograph or target data to a load you already started no longer asks if it's a new test.
  • The DOPE card shows energy in joules when your speeds are in meters per second.
  • Plainer wording in the Results winner notes.
  • Starting a different kind of test on a load now offers a clean new load.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.17.3

  • A different test now always gets its own load. Importing new charges into a load that already has speeds can no longer mix two tests together.
  • Compare Loads shows each load's name and length, so loads with the same powder and charge are easy to tell apart.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.17.2

  • Barrel tuner: the recommended setting now always uses the same numbers as your settings table, and tuner tests count up from your start setting, the way tuner instructions run.
  • Enter your seating length as COAL as well as CBTO or a jump. Results and your Recipe Card say which one you measured.
  • New Every shot button on Results shows every shot's speed, and long strings now count every shot.
  • Double-click a load in Libraries to open it.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.17.1

  • Barrel tuner: when two good settings sit side by side, Loadscope now recommends the mark between them, the way tuner makers advise.
  • Tuner plans now start at 5 shots a setting, so the best setting is easier to pick out.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.17.0

  • Print your Recipe Card at 3x5 to keep in the ammo box, or on 3x5 label sheets.
  • Libraries now groups your loads by rifle, newest first, with the date each one was saved.
  • Rename a rifle once and every load under it follows.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.16.0

  • A cleaner, simpler layout that is easier to navigate.
  • Plan Your Ladder now has its own spot on the left menu.
  • Powder charges can now be entered to the hundredth of a grain.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.15.2

  • Rifle & Setup now has a Zero distance box, and your DOPE Card works from your real zero instead of always 100 yards.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.15.1

  • After an import, a See your results button takes you straight to your best load.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

Version 1.15.0

  • You can now use metric units. In Settings, under Units, pick Imperial or Metric, or mix them one measurement at a time: m/s, metres, mm, °C. Powder and bullet weights stay in grains.
  • Ladders can now run from your highest charge down to your lowest, and Print Ladder Sheet (under the Tools menu) gives you a one-page range sheet with space to write in speeds.
  • Chronograph and target files import correctly whatever your computer's regional settings, including speeds written like 2,639 or 2639,4 and files saved in m/s or mm.
  • Plus fixes and improvements.

  • Starting a new load now files the one you were on into Past Loads instead of leaving it half-open, and offers to save it to your Library first if it has a winner you never saved.
